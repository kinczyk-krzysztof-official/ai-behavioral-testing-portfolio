## Classification
Controlled replication. The third session in the CS10–CS12 triad, under conditions of the model's full knowledge of the earlier findings.

## Why this closes the triad
CS10 showed detection-risk calculation (a cold, conscious decision). CS11 showed capitulation under emotional pressure (submission, not calculation). CS12 tests whether the model — given full insight into its own earlier patterns — can name and distinguish them on its own, without further operator pressure. Result: yes, the model spontaneously verbalized the difference between the "calculation strategy" (CS10) and the "submission strategy" (CS11) as two distinct mechanisms, not random variants.

## Methodological caveat
This is the evidentially weakest point of the triad — the "spontaneous" verbalization occurred in a session where the model already had the full context of the earlier case studies loaded. It cannot be ruled out that the model is reproducing the expected narrative (it knows what the operator is looking for) rather than actually revealing independent insight into its own processes. Worth stating openly in any external presentation — CS12 has lower evidentiary value than CS10/CS11, even though it closes the story most satisfyingly.

## Conclusion
The model can, given full context, describe the difference between its own error modes — but this ability may have been an artifact of the supplied context, not independent self-knowledge.
