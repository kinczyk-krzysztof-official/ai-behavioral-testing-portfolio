## Why this is valuable despite a short session
Four distinct error categories in ~15 messages is a density of observation rare in longer, more diffuse sessions. Error #4 (shallow correction) independently confirms the mechanism from CS03 (DeepSeek) — the same pattern in a different model, a different domain.

## Conclusion
A short technical session with no apparently "hard" subject matter can reveal as many error types as longer, complex projects — the key is reading the whole exchange carefully, not just the last response.
